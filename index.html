Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our lead generation services

Are leads exclusive? +

Yes, all our leads are exclusive to your business. We do not resell leads to multiple clients. Once a lead is delivered to you, it is removed from our active inventory to ensure you have the first opportunity to convert.

How do you verify leads? +

Every lead goes through a two-step verification process. First, we verify contact information via email confirmation. Second, our team conducts a brief phone verification to confirm interest and ensure the lead matches the requested criteria. Only verified leads are delivered to clients.

What industries do you support? +

We specialize in four primary verticals: insurance (home, auto, health, life), personal loans, mortgage/refinance, and solar installation. Our team has deep experience in these industries and understands the specific lead requirements for each.

What is minimum order? +

Our minimum order is 100 leads per month for new clients. This allows us to properly onboard your business and ensure lead quality meets your expectations. After the initial period, we can work with you to adjust volume based on your capacity and results.
