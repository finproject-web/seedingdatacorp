Is SeedingData a lender? +

No, SeedingData is not a lender. We are a free lead generation and matching service that connects consumers with lenders and financial service providers from our trusted network.

What We Do: We collect your information through our secure form and share it with potential lenders who may be able to help with your loan request.

All loan decisions, terms, and approval are determined directly by individual lenders based on their own criteria. We do not make credit decisions, provide loans, or influence lender decisions in any way.

Will this affect my credit score? +

Submitting your information through SeedingData will not directly affect your credit score. Our initial matching process does not involve a hard credit check.

However: When lenders review your application, they may perform a credit check. This could be a soft inquiry (which doesn't affect your score) or a hard inquiry (which may temporarily impact your score).

  • Soft Inquiry: Typically doesn't affect credit score
  • Hard Inquiry: May cause a temporary, small drop in score
  • Multiple Inquiries: Multiple hard inquiries in a short time can have a larger impact

Each lender has their own policies regarding credit checks. You can ask lenders about their credit check process before proceeding with any application.

How long does the process take? +

The SeedingData matching process is very fast, but the overall timeline depends on lender response times and your decisions.

  • Form Completion: 3-5 minutes
  • Our Processing: 1-2 minutes
  • Lender Contact: 5 minutes to 24 hours
  • Loan Funding: Varies by lender (typically 1-3 business days if approved)

Important: While our matching is fast, we cannot guarantee specific timelines for lender responses or loan funding, as these are controlled by individual lenders.

Some lenders offer same-day funding, while others may take several business days. The exact timing will be provided by each lender in their offer details.

What credit score is needed? +

There is no specific minimum credit score required to use SeedingData. We work with lenders who consider various credit backgrounds, from excellent to poor credit.

Credit Score Considerations: Different lenders have different criteria. Some specialize in working with consumers who have excellent credit, while others focus on helping those with fair or bad credit.

  • Excellent (750+): More options, potentially better rates
  • Good (700-749): Good range of options
  • Fair (650-699): Some lenders may work with you
  • Poor (600-649): Limited but available options
  • Bad (Below 600): Fewer options, but some lenders specialize in this range

No Guarantee: We cannot guarantee that any specific credit score will result in loan approval, as each lender has their own underwriting criteria and requirements.

Is my information safe? +

Yes, we take data security very seriously. We implement multiple layers of security to protect your information.

  • 256-bit SSL Encryption: Protects data during transmission
  • Secure Data Storage: Your information is stored in secure systems
  • Access Controls: Limited access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular Security Updates: We maintain up-to-date security measures

Sharing Policy: We only share your information with lenders in our network for the purpose of providing you with loan offers. We do not sell your information to unrelated third parties.

However, no security system is completely impenetrable. While we take reasonable precautions, you should be aware that all online services carry some level of risk.

Am I obligated to accept an offer? +

No, you are under absolutely no obligation to accept any loan offers presented to you through our service.

Your Choice Matters: You have complete control over the process. You can review all offers, ask questions, and decline any or all offers without any penalty or cost.

  • Review offers at your own pace
  • Ask lenders any questions you have
  • Decline offers that don't meet your needs
  • Take time to compare options
  • Walk away at any time

There is no pressure to accept any offer, and no consequences for declining. Our service is designed to help you explore your options, not to push you into any decision.

Why am I being contacted by multiple lenders? +

You're being contacted by multiple lenders because SeedingData works with a network of lenders, and we share your information with several potential matches simultaneously.

Benefits of Multiple Contacts: This approach increases your chances of finding a suitable loan option and allows you to compare different offers side by side.

  • More Options: Multiple lenders mean more potential loan offers
  • Comparison Shopping: Compare rates, terms, and conditions
  • Better Chances: If one lender declines, others may still approve
  • Competitive Offers: Lenders may offer better terms to compete for your business

If you wish to stop receiving communications, you can opt out directly with each lender or contact us for assistance.

How do I opt out of communications? +

You can opt out of communications at any time. Here are the different ways to manage your communication preferences:

  • Email: Click the "unsubscribe" link in marketing emails
  • Text Messages: Reply "STOP" to any text message
  • Phone Calls: Ask to be removed from call lists during conversations
  • Direct Contact: Reach out to lenders directly to opt out

SeedingData Opt-Out: To opt out of our communications specifically, contact us at privacy@seedingdata.com or call 1-800-SEED-DATA.

Please note that opting out of marketing communications may not stop transactional messages related to active loan applications or inquiries.

Is SeedingData really free? +

Yes, SeedingData is completely free for consumers. There is no charge to use our matching service or to submit your information.

How We Make Money: We may receive compensation from our lending partners when they successfully connect with qualified borrowers through our platform.

  • No application fees
  • No subscription charges
  • No hidden costs
  • No obligation to accept any offers

Our compensation does not affect your loan terms, rates, or the offers you receive from lenders. You will never pay anything to use SeedingData's services.

What happens to my information after I'm matched? +

After you submit your information, here's what happens:

  • Immediate Processing: Our system processes your information right away
  • Lender Matching: We match you with suitable lenders from our network
  • Information Sharing: Your data is shared only with matched lenders
  • Lender Contact: Lenders contact you directly with offers
  • Data Retention: We keep your information to improve our service and for compliance

Important: Once your information is shared with lenders, they have their own data retention and privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies directly.

You can request access to or deletion of your information by contacting us at privacy@seedingdata.com.
