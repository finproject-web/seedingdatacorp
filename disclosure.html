TCPA Consent Disclaimer

Important information about telephone consent and communications

Last Updated: January 2024

TCPA Compliance Notice

SeedingData Corp complies with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). All consumer leads are generated with proper express written consent before any autodialed calls or text messages are sent.

1. What is TCPA Consent?

The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) is a federal law that regulates telemarketing calls, auto-dialed calls, text messages, and prerecorded messages. At SeedingData Corp, we ensure all consumer leads are generated with proper TCPA consent.

Express Written Consent Required:

  • Consumers must provide express written consent to receive autodialed calls
  • Consent must be clear and unambiguous
  • Consumers must be informed of the purpose of the consent
  • Consent must be recorded with a timestamp
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase

2. Our Consent Collection Process

How We Obtain Consent: We use a clear, unambiguous consent process that meets all TCPA requirements.

Consent Process Steps:

  • Clear Disclosure: Consumers are informed they will receive calls/texts
  • Opt-In Mechanism: Consumers actively check a box to provide consent
  • Identity of Caller: Consumers know who will be contacting them
  • Phone Number Provided: Consumers provide their own phone number
  • Timestamp Recording: Exact time of consent is recorded

Consent Language Example:

"By checking this box, I agree to receive autodialed and pre-recorded telemarketing calls and text messages from SeedingData Corp and its partners regarding my inquiry at the phone number provided above. I understand that consent is not a condition of purchase."

3. Types of Communications Requiring Consent

Under TCPA regulations, certain types of communications require express written consent from consumers.

Communications Requiring Consent:

  • Autodialed Calls: Calls made using automatic telephone dialing systems
  • Prerecorded Messages: Pre-recorded voice messages
  • Text Messages (SMS): Marketing and informational text messages
  • Artificial/Prerecorded Voice: Voice calls using artificial or prerecorded voices

Communications NOT Requiring Consent:

  • Manual Calls: Calls made by a human operator
  • Emergency Calls: Calls for emergency purposes
  • Transactional Messages: Non-marketing informational messages
  • Existing Business Relationships: Some calls to existing customers (with limitations)

4. Consumer Rights and Opt-Out Options

Important: Consumers have the right to opt out of receiving communications at any time. We honor all opt-out requests promptly.

Opt-Out Methods:

  • Text Messages: Reply "STOP" to opt out of SMS messages
  • Phone Calls: Follow opt-out instructions provided during calls
  • Email: Click unsubscribe link or reply to opt out
  • Direct Contact: Contact us directly to manage preferences

Opt-Out Response Time:

  • Text message opt-outs: Within 10 business days
  • Phone call opt-outs: Immediate or within 24 hours
  • Email opt-outs: Within 10 business days
  • Direct requests: Within 10 business days

5. Business Client Responsibilities

Business clients who purchase leads from SeedingData Corp must maintain TCPA compliance when contacting consumers.

Client TCPA Responsibilities:

  • Honor Consent: Only contact consumers who have provided consent
  • Respect Opt-Outs: Immediately honor consumer opt-out requests
  • Comply with Time Restrictions: Follow calling time restrictions (8 AM to 9 PM local time)
  • Maintain Records: Keep records of consent and communications
  • Follow State Laws: Comply with state-specific TCPA regulations

Prohibited Practices:

  • Contacting consumers after opt-out requests
  • Using consent for purposes other than originally disclosed
  • Contacting consumers outside allowed calling hours
  • Failing to maintain consent records
  • Sharing consent with unauthorized third parties

6. Consent Record Retention

SeedingData Corp maintains records of TCPA consent for the required retention period and provides this documentation to business clients.

Record Retention:

  • Consent Records: Retained for 4 years as required by TCPA
  • Timestamp: Exact date and time of consent recorded
  • Consent Language: Full text of consent disclosure retained
  • Delivery Documentation: Proof of consent delivery to clients

Documentation Provided to Clients:

  • Consent timestamp for each lead
  • Copy of consent language used
  • Verification of consent authenticity
  • Opt-out history (if applicable)

7. State-Specific TCPA Regulations

In addition to federal TCPA requirements, some states have additional regulations that must be followed.

States with Additional Requirements:

  • Florida: Additional consent requirements for telemarketing
  • California: Stricter opt-out requirements
  • Washington: Additional disclosure requirements
  • Other States: Various state-specific regulations

Compliance Note: SeedingData Corp monitors state-specific TCPA regulations and ensures compliance with both federal and state requirements. Business clients are responsible for complying with regulations in the states where they operate.
