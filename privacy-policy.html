Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in our B2B lead generation services.

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

SeedingData Corp collects various types of information to provide lead generation services to businesses and to generate leads for our clients.

Business Client Information

  • Company name and business address
  • Contact name and business email
  • Business phone number
  • Lead type preferences and volume requirements
  • Business tax identification (if applicable)

Consumer Lead Information

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • State of residence
  • Interest category (insurance, loans, solar, mortgage)
  • TCPA consent and timestamp

Technical Information

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Referral source

2. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

Primary Purpose: To generate and deliver qualified leads to our business clients in the insurance, loans, solar, and mortgage industries.

Specific Uses Include:

  • Generating and verifying consumer leads
  • Delivering leads to business clients
  • Communicating with business clients about services
  • Providing customer support and service
  • Analyzing and improving lead quality
  • Marketing and promotional communications (with consent)
  • Compliance with legal obligations including TCPA
  • Fraud prevention and security

3. Sharing Information with Partners

Important: We share consumer lead information with our business clients who have purchased leads. By using our service, business clients consent to receiving leads that have been collected with proper TCPA consent.

We Share Consumer Leads With:

  • Business Clients: Who have purchased leads for their marketing and sales purposes
  • Service Providers: Third-party companies that help us operate our business (e.g., software providers, data processors)
  • Verification Partners: Who assist in verifying lead quality and contact information
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights

All consumer leads are delivered exclusively to the purchasing business client and are not resold to multiple parties.

4. TCPA Compliance and Consumer Consent

TCPA Compliance: All consumer leads are generated with proper TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) consent. Consumers provide express written consent to receive autodialed calls and text messages at the phone number provided.

Consent Process:

  • Express Consent: Consumers explicitly opt-in to receive communications
  • Clear Disclosure: Consumers are informed they will receive calls/texts
  • Timestamp Recording: Consent timestamp is recorded for each lead
  • Opt-Out Information: Consumers are informed of their right to opt-out

Consent is not a condition of purchase for any service. Consumers can opt out of communications at any time.

5. Data Security Practices

We implement reasonable security measures to protect information, but no security system is completely impenetrable.

Security Measures Include:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure data storage practices
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security monitoring
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Compliance with industry standards

We cannot guarantee absolute security of information. By using our service, you acknowledge and accept these risks.

6. Your Rights (Including California Residents)

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

General Rights:

  • Access to your personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of your information (where legally permitted)
  • Opt-out of certain data sharing and marketing

California Residents (CCPA Rights):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Consumer Opt-Out Rights

Consumers whose information is included in leads have the right to opt out of future communications.

Opt-Out Methods:

  • Reply "STOP" to text messages to opt out of SMS
  • Follow opt-out instructions in phone calls
  • Contact us directly to request removal from lead lists
  • Submit a privacy request through our contact information

We honor opt-out requests within a reasonable time frame, typically within 10 business days.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for different periods depending on the purpose and legal requirements.

Retention Periods:

  • Business Client Information: Retained while client relationship is active plus 3 years
  • Consumer Lead Data: Retained for 2 years from generation date or until delivered to client
  • TCPA Consent Records: Retained for 4 years as required by law
  • Marketing Communications: Retained until opt-out or 2 years of inactivity
